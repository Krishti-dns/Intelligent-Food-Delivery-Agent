A* heuristic

f(n) = g(n) + h(n)

g(n) is travel time so far. h(n) is Manhattan distance to the destination. BFS and DFS show traversal order; A* uses the road times to find a least-time route.

Priority rule
Urgency points + deadline points + 1 point for perishable food. High: 5+, Medium: 3–4, Low: 0–2.
